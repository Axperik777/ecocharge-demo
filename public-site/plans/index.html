Calculation method and access to funds

The monthly amount is your capital multiplied by the selected plan rate. The seven-day estimate uses the actual calendar months and the same calculation as your account. It does not compound. Credits are recorded after completed seven-day periods.

A withdrawal window is not a commitment to return the invested capital. Review the holding period, fees and exit terms for the specific project before confirming participation.

How plans are calculated

Plan questions

Does entering a larger amount change my rate?+

No. Choose a plan first. Changing your contribution changes the dollar estimate at the selected monthly rate.

When can I request a withdrawal?+

Withdrawal requests open every 4 days according to the account calendar. Weekly accruals remain weekly.
