How plans are calculated

Capital × monthly rate, allocated across the calendar days. The weekly estimate covers the next seven days.

Choose the tariff and amount together. Each tariff has its own minimum. Your manager can save the agreed plan in your account.

  1. 1

    Choose your amount

    Start from $250 or discuss your own amount from $1,000.

  2. 2

    Review the weekly amount

    The rate is monthly; credits are recorded weekly. Calendar months have 28–31 days.

  3. 3

    Activate the agreed plan

    After funding is confirmed, activate your plan in the account.

Plan questions

Does entering a larger amount change my rate?+

No. Review and select a tariff first. Changing the capital changes the dollar estimate at the selected monthly rate.

When can I request a withdrawal?+

Withdrawal requests open every 4 days according to the account calendar. Weekly accruals remain weekly.
