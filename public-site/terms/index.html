Operating risks

Equipment downtime, lower demand and operating costs can reduce available funds. Investment capital may be at risk; the project establishes no capital protection.

Does a sample withdrawal prove I can withdraw real money?+

No. It is a local interface simulation. It does not prove liquidity, custody arrangements, bank processing or a contractual right to withdraw.

What supports the Charge company obligation?+

The signed Charge agreement must define the selected 13–16% calendar-month company obligation, its funding order and the four-day settlement. This workspace is not connected to operating evidence, verified revenue, reserve balances or real payments; the interface alone does not prove funding or payment.

What do the reference guides cover?+

The reference guides cover participation rights, fees, withdrawals and risks. They explain what a real agreement would need to address.
