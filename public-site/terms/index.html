Does a sample withdrawal prove I can withdraw real money?+

No. It is a local interface simulation. It does not prove liquidity, custody arrangements, bank processing or a contractual right to withdraw.

Are monthly rates supported by operating results?+

No operating evidence is connected. The monthly rates shown inside the account are model assumptions, not verified or guaranteed returns. More selected stations alone does not justify a higher return.

What do the reference guides cover?+

The reference guides cover participation rights, fees, withdrawals and risks. They explain what a real agreement would need to address.
