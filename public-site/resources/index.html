Questions & answers

What happens after I register?+

Your account includes the station map, plans, documents and messages with your manager.

When can I request a withdrawal?+

You can request a withdrawal from your available balance 4 days after activation, then 4 days after each approved withdrawal. Earnings are credited after complete 7-day periods; the first earnings credit is not available on day 4. The date is shown in your account. Requests use your available balance.

Can I deposit or withdraw real money?+

No. Financial activity is simulated. Real payments are not connected.

How is the weekly allocation calculated?+

The workspace applies the selected monthly percentage to invested plan capital. You can choose your amount and inspect the complete calculation inside your account. It does not add an extra credit for every charging vehicle.

How is energy allocated in this model?+

The model routes production to EV charging, commercial clients and the grid. The split describes energy, not ownership or a plan rate. Supporting asset and contract documents must be reviewed.

Are the listed stations owned by EcoGrid?+

Ownership has not been established. Stations and photos are third-party public references. Operator names identify the public listings and do not imply a partnership or endorsement.

How do messages work?+

Messages appear in the team workspace. External support channels are not connected.

Where is my information saved?+

Your profile and activity stay in this browser. No email is sent and no server account is created. Use sample details. Clearing site data resets your workspace. Another browser or device has separate data.

What should I verify before investing real money?+

Verify the legal issuer, offering terms, eligibility, ownership evidence, revenue records, fees, withdrawal rules and risk disclosures. Sample documents and a working interface do not establish an investment right or prove returns.
