Project document scope

What each project document covers

Company registration

Legal entity, jurisdiction, registration number, authority to sign and authorized representatives.

Shared operating foundation

Documented rights to the energy sites, grid connections, charging equipment, solar and storage assets, compute hardware, cooling, network and operating contracts used by each project.

Charge participation agreement

The selected Charge plan, company obligation, funding order, company payable, settlement, client rights and capital-exit conditions.

Charge operating evidence

Dated charging receipts, verified Charge revenue, the eligible company reserve, electricity and site costs, service expenses, downtime and settlement records.

Solar equipment quote & service scope

Site assessment, system design, equipment, installation, permissions, final price, warranty, service and delivery assumptions for the selected property.

Solar participation proposal

The specified site or assets, output and sales assumptions, operating costs, legal instrument, client rights, distribution rules, risks and exit conditions.

Compute infrastructure participation proposal

The legal instrument, relevant assets or capacity, utilization revenue, energy, cooling, network, service and replacement costs, client rights, distribution rules, risks and exit conditions.

Project fees & payment handling

Project-specific prices, fees, deductions, taxes, the legal recipient of client funds, custody or invoicing arrangements and payment processing.

Project risk disclosures

Operating, delivery, demand, downtime, illiquidity and counterparty risks for the selected project and request type.

Offering, service & eligibility

Which clients may buy equipment or discuss participation, and the legal basis and restrictions for each offer.

Questions & answers

What happens after I register?+

Your account keeps the project you selected, Academy context, documents and messages with your manager. Project-specific tools appear only when they apply to that project.

When can I request a Charge withdrawal?+

After confirmed Charge activation, the fixed company obligation accrues continuously at 13–16% for a full UTC calendar month. Funded profit is fixed every 4 days and can be requested for withdrawal once every 4 days. The next date and available amount are shown in your account; an uncovered amount remains company payable. Plan capital follows separate exit terms.

How is the Charge available payout calculated?+

The selected fixed Charge company obligation is 13–16% for a full UTC calendar month on Charge plan capital. Accrual starts after confirmed activation and funded profit is fixed every 4 days. Only funded settled profit is available to withdraw; an uncovered amount remains company payable. Funding, settlement and payout terms follow the signed Charge agreement.

Which terms apply to Solar?+

A Solar equipment request needs a final site-specific quote. A Solar participation request needs its own proposal and agreement. The selected Solar format determines the scope, price, rights, risks and next step.

What defines Compute participation?+

A separate proposal and agreement define the infrastructure, revenue from paid workloads, operating costs, rights, reporting, risks and exit conditions. Charge terms do not apply.

How do shared assets affect project terms?+

Shared engineering, sites, energy, storage, monitoring or operations may support more than one project where technically possible. That does not merge their revenue, costs, rights, risks or agreements.

Are the listed stations owned by EcoGrid?+

Ownership has not been established. Stations and photos are third-party public references. Operator names identify the public listings and do not imply a partnership or endorsement.

Where is my information saved?+

Your account keeps the selected project, Academy progress, documents and conversations together. Storage and access follow the published privacy terms.

What should I verify before signing or paying?+

Verify the contracting entity, selected project and request type, scope, price or economics, eligibility, asset or service evidence, fees, access and exit rules, and risk disclosures. Only the applicable signed project documents create contractual rights. Payment follows confirmed official instructions.
