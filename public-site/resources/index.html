Questions & answers

What happens after I register?+

Your account keeps the project you selected, Academy context, documents and messages with your manager. Project-specific tools appear only when they apply to that project.

When can I request a Charge withdrawal?+

After confirmed Charge activation, the fixed company obligation accrues continuously at 13–16% for a full UTC calendar month. Funded profit is fixed every 4 days and can be requested for withdrawal once every 4 days. The next date and available amount are shown in your account; an uncovered amount remains company payable. Plan capital follows separate exit terms.

How is the Charge available payout calculated?+

The workspace illustrates the selected fixed Charge company obligation of 13–16% for a full UTC calendar month on Charge plan capital. Accrual starts after confirmed activation and funded profit is fixed every 4 days. Only funded settled profit is available to withdraw; an uncovered amount remains company payable. The interface is not a signed agreement or proof of funding.

Which terms apply to Solar?+

A Solar equipment request needs a final site-specific quote. A Solar participation request needs its own proposal and agreement. The selected Solar format determines the scope, price, rights, risks and next step.

Does a Compute capacity order mean project participation?+

No. Buying managed GPU capacity is a service request. Participation in AI-computing infrastructure is a separate request with a separate legal instrument, economics, rights and risks.

How do shared assets affect project terms?+

Shared engineering, sites, energy, storage, monitoring or operations may support more than one project where technically possible. That does not merge their revenue, costs, rights, risks or agreements.

Are the listed stations owned by EcoGrid?+

Ownership has not been established. Stations and photos are third-party public references. Operator names identify the public listings and do not imply a partnership or endorsement.

How do messages work?+

Messages appear in the team workspace. External support channels are not connected.

Where is my information saved?+

When registration is connected, your questionnaire and account are stored on the server. The administrator reviews the questionnaire and forwards it to your assigned manager. Staff cannot see your password. While sign-in and registration are unavailable, these forms do not submit or save your details. The separate sample workspace uses browser storage.

What should I verify before signing or paying?+

Verify the contracting entity, selected project and request type, scope, price or economics, eligibility, asset or service evidence, fees, access and exit rules, and risk disclosures. Sample documents and a working interface do not create contractual rights or prove a result.
