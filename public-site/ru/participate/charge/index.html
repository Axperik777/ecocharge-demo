What you receive. Where the result comes from. What can go wrong.

  1. 01

    What you receive

    A written Charge participation agreement with the selected monthly plan, account settlement, rights and exit terms.

  2. 02

    Source of the result

    Drivers pay for charging. Forty-two percent of verified Charge revenue funds company obligations first; an eligible shortfall moves to the company reserve.

  3. 03

    Main risk

    The company obligation does not guarantee capital repayment. Payment depends on verified funding, the reserve and the company’s ability to perform.

  4. 04

    Next step

    Choose at least three reference stations, then review the current proposal and agreement with a specialist.

What the Charge agreement fixes: one calendar model and a clear funding order.

$250.00 · 13% per full UTC calendar month

Full 30-day month
$250.00 × 13% = $32.50
First 4 calendar days
$32.50 × 4 ÷ 30 ≈ $4.33

A 28- or 31-day month changes the four-day amount slightly. Only funded and settled profit becomes withdrawable; the example does not remove company, capital or counterparty risk.

AccrualContinuous

SettlementEvery 4 days

Funding orderRevenue → reserve

The 42% verified-revenue pool funds obligations first; an eligible shortfall is covered by the company reserve.

If funding is still insufficient

The uncovered amount remains company payable. It does not disappear, but the obligation does not remove counterparty or capital risk.

Optional Charge AI: exactly +1 percentage point

EcoGrid AI · optional service

When Charge AI is confirmed and active, it adds exactly +1 percentage point to the selected plan rate for 30 days. After that period, the base rate applies again.

Activation is confirmed individually for a 30-day period after the AI terms and the related payment are verified. EcoCoin is only a planned Academy scenario in this build and is not issued, credited or used for activation. There is no automatic renewal. The uplift is not 1% of revenue or a payout guarantee.
