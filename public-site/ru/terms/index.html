Access, fees & risks

Use the project and request type to find the right commercial terms. Company-wide information does not replace a project agreement.

Terms every request should identify

Shared engineering does not create shared commercial terms. Start by naming the project, request type and contracting entity.

Project and request type

The document must name Charge, Solar or Compute and state whether it covers equipment, a service or capacity order, or project participation.

Project must be named

Your legal rights

The contracting entity, instrument or service, client rights and responsibilities must be stated for the selected project and request.

Project agreement required

Duration, renewal and exit

Delivery or activation dates, service duration, renewal, cancellation and, where participation applies, capital-exit conditions.

Written timing required

Project price, fees and deductions

The price basis, operating deductions, service or platform fees, taxes and any other client costs.

Project fee schedule required

Who handles the money

The legal recipient, account holder and any custody, invoicing or payment arrangements for this request.

Payment arrangements required

Project-specific risks

The written terms must explain the operating, delivery, counterparty and financial risks that apply to the selected project and request.

Project risk disclosure required

Financial and environmental claims both need evidence.

Project documents should separate goals, assumptions and measured results for revenue, energy, equipment and waste.

EcoGrid Charge · participation

Charge company obligation and settlement

Only Charge uses the selected 13–16% company obligation for a full UTC calendar month. The signed Charge agreement must define continuous accrual, settlement every 4 days, the order in which 42% of verified Charge revenue and an eligible company reserve fund the obligation, company payable, and separate capital-exit conditions.

A confirmed Charge AI service adds +1 percentage point only for its confirmed active period. It is not automatic and does not change Solar or Compute terms.

EcoGrid Solar · two request types

Equipment order or Solar participation

Equipment: the final quote must define the address, system scope, equipment, installation, permissions, price, warranty, service and delivery assumptions.

Participation: a separate proposal must define the site or assets, energy output and sales assumptions, operating costs, client rights, distribution rules, risks and exit conditions.

Review the Solar request
EcoGrid Compute · two request types

GPU capacity or infrastructure participation

Capacity request: the written proposal must define the workload, accelerator class, requested capacity, location, availability, metering, data and security boundaries, support, price basis and delivery dependencies.

Participation request: a separate infrastructure proposal must define the legal instrument, relevant assets or capacity, utilization revenue, energy, cooling, network, service and replacement costs, client rights, distribution rules, risks and exit conditions. A capacity order is not project participation.

Review the Compute request

How to read project risk

Equipment delivery, service availability, demand, operating costs and counterparty performance affect different requests in different ways. An equipment order, a capacity service and project participation are not interchangeable.

Does the interface create a contractual right?+

No. An account, calculator, document preview or request form does not establish delivery, liquidity, ownership, a payout right or capital protection. Those rights must appear in the applicable signed document.

Which terms apply when I change projects?+

The terms follow the project and request you explicitly choose. Viewing another project does not transfer rates, settlement, ownership or service conditions from the previous project.

What do the reference guides cover?+

The document register separates company evidence, Charge, Solar and Compute documents. Each file remains pending until it is supplied and reviewed.
