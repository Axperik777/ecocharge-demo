Revenue & costs

Set charging demand, energy use, selling price, energy cost and fixed expenses. These inputs calculate a 30-day operating result before financing, tax or distributions; they are separate from this public station record and from a client return.

What this calculation includes

Energy = daily sessions × energy per session × 30 days. Receipts = energy × charging price. Electricity cost = energy × electricity price. Other costs represent the selected allowance for site expenses, maintenance and equipment wear.

Demand charges, payment fees, financing, tax, capital expenditure and distribution rules are not calculated separately. Actual costs come from operator records. The production split and plan rates are not applied to this operating calculation.
