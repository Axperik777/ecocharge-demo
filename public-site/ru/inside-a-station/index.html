Revenue & costs

Own solar generation and battery storage are the energy source in this example. All inputs are adjustable assumptions, not this public station’s measured costs. The 30-day operating example does not calculate a client return.

What this example includes

Energy = daily sessions × energy per session × 30 days. Receipts = energy × charging price. Electricity cost = energy × electricity price. Other costs represent an assumed combined allowance for site expenses, maintenance and equipment wear.

Demand charges, payment fees, financing, tax, capital expenditure and distribution rules are not modeled separately. Real costs require operator records. The production split and plan rates are not applied to this operating example.
