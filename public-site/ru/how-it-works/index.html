How EcoGrid works

EcoGrid connects energy generation, storage and demand. Every project is reviewed through two equal lenses: how it creates economic value and how it manages energy, equipment and its full life cycle.

EcoGrid Charge — EV charging infrastructure

Economic value

Drivers pay for charging sessions. The signed plan defines the company payment obligation, its funding and the four-day settlement.

Environmental responsibility

EVs have no tailpipe emissions while driving. The site still needs transparent data on its electricity mix, utilization, efficiency and equipment life.

EcoGrid Solar — Solar generation and storage

Economic value

Solar plants produce electricity for sale or on-site use. Generation, energy-sale terms and operating costs determine the project result.

Environmental responsibility

Panels generate electricity without burning fuel on site. Materials, service life, repair and end-of-life handling remain part of the assessment.

Explore the project →

EcoGrid Mining — Rented computing cells

Economic value

Clients rent a computing cell on an EcoGrid farm and receive the digital-asset result under the rental agreement. Market conditions, uptime and service costs determine the result; the client does not buy the miner.

Environmental responsibility

EcoGrid Solar generation and storage power the miners and cooling systems. Efficiency, equipment life, repair and electronic-waste handling remain the operator’s responsibility.

How the energy flows

Model, not telemetry

The EcoGrid model combines solar generation in the U.S. and Canada, battery storage and three independent energy channels: EV charging, commercial energy agreements and grid sales. Actual routing depends on the site, demand, tariffs, grid constraints and contracts; there is no single fixed percentage split across the ecosystem. Storage shifts part of daytime generation into the evening and night.

EcoGrid AI

EcoGrid AI is our shared operating intelligence across every project. It compares generation, demand, tariffs and equipment condition to help allocate energy where it delivers more value and reduce avoidable costs. Operators set the limits and approve operating decisions.

A separate EcoGrid Charge rule

42% of verified Charge revenue is applied first to the fixed company payment obligations. If that amount is insufficient, an eligible company reserve covers the shortfall; any uncovered amount remains recorded as company payable and is never silently written off. This rule does not present the remainder as a client or company ownership share.

After agreement activation, the selected Charge plan creates a fixed company payment obligation of 13–16% per UTC calendar month, accrued continuously by time. The obligation is not reduced by weak station revenue, but its fulfilment depends on the company and its reserve; it is not a guarantee of capital repayment.
