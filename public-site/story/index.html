Where it started

A charger is more than hardware.

James Robert Whitaker was born March 12, 1968, in Youngstown, Ohio, the son of a mill-town electrician and a school secretary. The house smelled like coffee and burnt insulation. His father, Robert, came home with knuckles that would not come clean. Two words were the whole religion: respect current.

Jim finished Boardman High in 1986 and went straight into an IBEW apprenticeship in the Mahoning Valley. College could wait. Live panels would not. He spent four years learning that electricity does not care about your plans. It cares about torque, grounding, and whether you showed up.

In 1998 he painted his own name on a van: Whitaker Electric. Grocery remodels. Storefront lighting. A stamping plant that called at 2 a.m. The work was unfashionable. It paid the mortgage. In 1994 he had married Diane, a school administrator with no patience for men who talk bigger than their invoices.

Move to Clermont

Florida arrived the way it arrives for a lot of Ohio families — a winter job, then another, then a sister of Diane’s already living near Clermont. In 2014 they moved. Jim was forty-six, too old to be a prospect and too stubborn to retire into a golf joke. Central Florida was pouring concrete. Somebody had to wire it.

The first charging job came in 2011, before the move, for a small delivery fleet that had bought a handful of early electric vans and could not keep them on the road. The hardware was the easy part. The hard part was the bill, the downtime, the Saturday cable that failed in August heat. Jim understood that in his bones. A charger is not a product. It is a small utility that has to work at 9 p.m. when nobody from a pitch deck is answering the phone.

Why EcoGrid

One name. A connected energy system.

Grid means an energy network. EcoGrid connects generation, storage and use through EcoGrid Solar, EcoGrid Storage and EcoGrid Charge. EcoGrid Mining is a separate computing direction with its own equipment and economics. The name describes the connection between these activities.

The years

The years behind the work.

An apprenticeship in 1986. A business in 1998. Charging in 2011. EcoGrid in 2018. Different milestones, one working life.

  1. 1968

    Youngstown

    Born to Robert and Helen Whitaker. A mill town that taught him work is what you do with your hands when the mill is loud.

  2. 1986

    The apprenticeship

    Out of high school and into the hall. Four years of panels, conduit, and men who did not repeat themselves.

  3. 1994

    Diane

    Married. Two children later: Sarah, a nurse in Orlando, and Thomas, a third-generation electrician. The family still eats Sunday dinner in Clermont.

  4. 1998

    Whitaker Electric

    His name went on the invoice. Whitaker Electric took on commercial and industrial electrical work, years before charging became a separate business.

  5. 2011

    First chargers

    A twelve-truck delivery fleet in Central Florida. Hardware was a weekend. Operations became the next decade.

  6. 2014

    Move to Clermont

    The family moved. Jim opened a Florida shop. Charging work grew because the corridor grew — not because a slide said so.

  7. 2018

    EcoGrid

    The charging business became its own company. Same man. Clearer books. A rule: show the electricity cost the way a utility would.

  8. 2020

    The empty hours

    Retail lots went quiet. Delivery fleets did not. He learned a station has to survive the weeks nobody poses in front of it.

  9. 2022

    Open books

    Site hosts received operating reports they could read without a banker. That discipline is the seed of everything public now.

  10. 2026

    Four directions under EcoGrid

    EcoGrid Solar, EcoGrid Storage, EcoGrid Charge and EcoGrid Mining share one brand. Energy participation, mining participation and equipment purchases have separate terms and client journeys.

How EcoGrid works

Follow the energy. Understand the revenue.

Solar power and storage support three energy revenue channels: EV charging, commercial supply and grid sales. EcoGrid Mining has a separate calculation: coin revenue minus electricity, hosting and other operating costs. Equipment purchases do not activate an energy plan.

  1. 01

    Solar & storage

    Solar panels generate electricity. Batteries store it for later demand.

  2. 02

    Three revenue channels

    Drivers pay for charging. Businesses buy energy under commercial agreements. Around 43% of modeled generation goes to the grid.

  3. 03

    Costs, then participation

    Maintenance, site costs and reserves are deducted before the operating result. Your rights and distribution rules are defined by the agreement.

The principles

The work has to stand up to questions.

The story centers on practical experience, understandable costs, and responsibility for the site. These are the six principles behind it.

  1. A man with a town

    Jim Whitaker lives in Clermont. You can find the town. You can find the years. A logo is not a person.

  2. The trade came first

    Forty years in electrical work. A business owner since 1998. Charging grew out of the trade.

  3. We show the power bill

    If electricity is the largest cost, it belongs on the first page, not in a footnote after the hope.

  4. No overnight fortune

    Stations have empty hours. Equipment fails. We talk about that before anyone asks us to.

  5. Family still in the work

    Diane kept the books when the van was the office. Thomas is in the trade. Sunday dinner is not a brand campaign.

  6. Thursdays on site

    Jim still walks stations. If a pedestal is dark, that is his problem before it is anyone else’s story.

A letter from Jim

If you are reading this, you have probably heard enough big promises.

Clermont, FloridaSeptember 2026

I will not add another future. I am an electrician. I became a businessman because the invoices had my name on them. I formed EcoGrid because too many people were selling chargers the way you sell gadgets: unpack, plug, pose, disappear.

A charging station is not a gadget. It is a small utility. It has an electricity bill that does not care about your enthusiasm. It has a broken cable on a Saturday in August. It has a driver who just wants to get home to a kid, a shift, or a quiet house.

I have been around live current since I was eighteen. My father was an electrician in Youngstown. He came home tired. He came home honest. That is still the job.

The current EcoGrid model connects solar generation and battery storage with three sources of revenue: EV charging, energy supplied to businesses and surplus energy sold to the grid. Equipment and operating costs come first. The result is reported separately from the client’s plan calculation.

I still walk sites on Thursdays. If you want to talk, that is where I am.

Respect current.

Jim Whitaker

Founder & Chief Executive Officer, EcoGrid

Questions about the story.

Who runs EcoGrid?+

James R. Whitaker, founder and CEO. He is a tradesman from Youngstown, Ohio, who has lived in Clermont, Florida since 2014. He started in an electrical apprenticeship in 1986, ran Whitaker Electric from 1998, and formed EcoGrid in 2018 to operate charging as a business rather than a hardware pitch.

How long has the company been operating?+

EcoGrid the brand was founded in 2018. The charging work started in 2011. The underlying electrical business has carried Jim’s name since 1998. The man has been around live current for forty years. Those clocks are different, and we keep them honest.

How does EcoGrid make money?+

The current EcoGrid model connects solar generation and battery storage with three sources of revenue: EV charging, energy supplied to businesses and surplus energy sold to the grid. Equipment and operating costs come first. The result is reported separately from the client’s plan calculation.

What would support this story?+

The narrative puts a name, a town, and a trade behind the project. Those details still need supporting records. Company documents, operating statements, electricity costs, and downtime records would let a reader check the claims. A biography alone is not proof.

Is this an investment offer?+
