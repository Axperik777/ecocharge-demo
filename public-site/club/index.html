Before you join

Can I join without investing?

Yes. The Academy helps with registration and community access. Investing and using paid project AI are separate decisions.

How do I get started?

Talk to the Academy first. Ask for a project demonstration or help joining the community. If you already have an account, continue in its Academy section.

Are all events available now?

The event program is being prepared. Confirmed events will have a date, host, location or online link, and participation terms. Joining the Club does not automatically book an event.

What does the welcome gift cover?

500 EcoCoin can cover the planned 30-day project AI mode costing 472 EcoCoin. Receiving the gift requires no investment; using the mode requires participation in the selected project.

Do I have to invite anyone?

No. Join for your own interests. Invitations are optional and have separate terms.
