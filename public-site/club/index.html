Before you join

Can I join without investing?

Yes. You can join for the community, AI and member benefits. Investing in an EcoGrid project is a separate decision.

Do I need to know how to use AI?

No. Bring one practical question, try a clear example and learn step by step. You can ask for help in the community.

Is there a schedule I have to follow?

No. Most conversations happen in comments that you can read and reply to at any time. Occasional online sessions and recordings add to the experience.

Do I have to invite anyone?

No. Join for your own interests. If you choose to invite a friend, the invitation program has separate terms.

What else is planned for members?

Partner privileges and a travel insurance program are in preparation. Insurance starts only after a policy is issued and does not cover investment losses.
