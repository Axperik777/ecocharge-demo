Before you join

Who is the Club for?

For people who want to discuss responsible energy, technology, AI and their own ideas with others. You can begin by reading and join the conversation later.

How do I get started?

Choose a community channel, read the current topics and introduce yourself when you feel comfortable. The Academy is available when you want a structured lesson.

Are all events available now?

The event program is being prepared. Confirmed events will have a date, host, location or online link, and participation terms. Joining the Club does not automatically book an event.

Do I have to speak right away?

No. Read the discussions, save a useful idea and reply only when you have something to add. Quiet participation is welcome too.

Do I have to invite anyone?

No. The Club is valuable on its own; invite only people who genuinely care about the community topics.
