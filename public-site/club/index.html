A few things to know

Can I join without investing?

Yes. The community, online seminars, learning and member gifts are reasons to join in their own right. Investing in a project is optional and a separate decision.

How do I get my EcoCoin gift?

The welcome program includes an EcoCoin gift after membership is confirmed. The amount and delivery terms are provided with your welcome. No project deposit or invitation of friends is required.

Do I need to attend in person?

No. The Club is centered on online conversations, seminars and digital materials. Join from your phone or computer, and use recordings when a live session does not fit your schedule.

Do I have to invite anyone?

No. Come for the people, ideas and activities that interest you. If you choose to invite a friend, the invitation program has separate terms.

What else is planned for members?

Partner privileges and a travel insurance program are in preparation. Confirmed offers will appear in your account. Insurance starts only after a policy is issued and does not cover investment losses.
