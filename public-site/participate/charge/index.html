Questions to settle before you participate.

What am I requesting on this page?

A review of one current Charge project, its funding path, evidence, risks and written terms. Submitting the form does not register you, move money or activate a plan.

Do the station photos prove EcoGrid ownership or revenue?

No. They are public market references. Ownership, operating rights, revenue and reserve funding must be verified separately for the current EcoGrid project.

Is the 13–16% obligation a guaranteed cash payout?

No. It is the company obligation defined by the written plan. Only profit funded and recorded at settlement becomes withdrawable, and company, capital and counterparty risk remain.

What does “every four days” mean?

Every four days, the funded portion of accrued profit is calculated and recorded. Only that amount becomes available for withdrawal under the agreement. This is not a new earnings promise and does not prove that revenue or reserve funding is available.

What happens if verified revenue is not enough?

The 42% verified-revenue pool funds obligations first. The company reserve covers a shortfall only when it is eligible under the written agreement. Any uncovered amount remains recorded as company payable but is not available for withdrawal unless later funded and recorded at settlement. This order does not eliminate risk.

Where are duration, fees, eligibility, capital and exit terms confirmed?

In the proposal and agreement for the current project. Review those written terms before making any decision.

Is Charge AI included?

EcoGrid AI · optional service

When Charge AI is confirmed and active, it adds exactly +1 percentage point to the selected plan rate for 30 days. After that period, the base rate applies again.

Activation is confirmed individually for a 30-day period after the AI terms and the related payment are verified. There is no automatic renewal. The uplift is not 1% of revenue or a payout guarantee.
