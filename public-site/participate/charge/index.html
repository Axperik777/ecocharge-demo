What happens before you decide.

What am I applying for?

You are applying to explore a potential investment in an EcoGrid Charge project built around company-owned and operated charging stations. The application starts the review process; it does not complete an investment.

What happens after I apply?

EcoGrid Academy—our project walkthrough team—calls to confirm your interest and current Charge availability. If you choose to continue, the team completes secure registration and opens your client account.

How will the project be explained?

After registration, choose a guided account walkthrough or the same explanation by phone. You decide only after the operating model, account and next step are clear. If you choose to participate, the confirmed funding step follows.

How does Charge create revenue?

Drivers pay for completed charging sessions at EcoGrid-owned stations. EcoGrid reconciles session revenue with the operating costs of the charging infrastructure.
