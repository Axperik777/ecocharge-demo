EcoGrid Charge
Discuss Charge

Charging infrastructure with clear economics and responsible energy use.

EcoGrid Charge is the EV charging infrastructure participation project. After agreement activation, the selected plan creates a fixed company payment obligation of 13–16% per UTC calendar month. It accrues continuously and is settled every four days. Forty-two percent of verified Charge revenue funds these obligations first; the company reserve covers an eligible shortfall, while uncovered debt remains recorded.

Why this matters now

Changing fuel costs. A reason to look at charging.

Higher gasoline and diesel prices give drivers another reason to compare electric vehicles with fuel-powered cars. EVs have no tailpipe emissions while driving. Their full impact still depends on how electricity and equipment are produced and used; Charge focuses on the infrastructure that serves those drivers.

The opportunity is in paid charging sessions. Site usage, electricity prices and operating costs determine the project’s result.

The business in brief

Where revenue comes from. What affects the result.

Payment funding

Four-day settlement

The fixed 13–16% calendar-month obligation accrues continuously. Funded profit becomes withdrawable at settlement; any uncovered amount remains company payable.

What happens after my request?

The Academy explains Charge and demonstrates the account. Then it helps you register. You review the Charge proposal and documents in your account; funding comes only after you decide to participate.

What determines whether the obligation is paid?

The agreement fixes the company obligation; weak station revenue does not reduce it. Actual payment still depends on verified Charge revenue, the company reserve and the company’s ability to perform. Any uncovered amount remains company payable, but this does not remove counterparty or capital risk.
