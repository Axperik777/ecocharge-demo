  1. 01

    What you are considering

    Participation would be contractual—not ownership of a station or EcoGrid. A project-specific agreement must state your contribution, how it is used, what contractual claim you receive, who carries the obligation, the term, fees, reporting, risks and capital-exit conditions.

  2. 02

    How the model is funded

    Drivers pay for completed charging sessions. Forty-two percent of verified Charge revenue funds company obligations first; an eligible shortfall may be covered by the company reserve.

This illustrates the obligation formula. It is not a typical result and does not prove demand, revenue or reserve funding.

First 4 calendar days

Questions to settle before any decision.

What am I requesting on this page?

A specialist-led walkthrough of the Charge model and, after availability is confirmed, the evidence status for one identified project. Submitting the form does not register you, move money or activate a plan.

Is the 13–16% obligation a guaranteed cash payout?

No. It is the company obligation defined by the written plan. Only profit funded and recorded at settlement becomes withdrawable, and company, capital and counterparty risk remain.

What happens if verified revenue is not enough?

The 42% verified-revenue pool funds obligations first. The company reserve covers a shortfall only when it is eligible under the written agreement. Any uncovered amount remains recorded as company payable but is not available for withdrawal unless later funded and recorded at settlement. This order does not eliminate risk.

What happens to the remaining revenue?

This landing page does not assign it. The project economics and signed terms must explain the treatment of all revenue, operating costs, reserves and other obligations.

Can I withdraw capital every four days?

No. The four-day schedule applies only to profit that has been funded and recorded. Capital-exit conditions must appear in the project-specific signed agreement.

Does the public station catalog prove an EcoGrid project?

No. It provides market context only. Project identity, rights, revenue, reserve and operating responsibility require separate project-specific evidence.
