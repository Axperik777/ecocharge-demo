BUILT FROM OPERATING EXPERIENCE

Charge grew out of operating infrastructure—not a financial spreadsheet.

In 2011, a small delivery fleet could not keep its early electric vans on the road. Installing the hardware was the easy part. The electricity bill, downtime and a failed cable on a hot Saturday were the real job.

A charger is not just hardware. It is a small utility that has to work when the driver needs it.

That lesson became EcoGrid operating discipline: connect power, equipment, monitoring and field service before reconciling the record.

What to know before you get started.

What happens after I send the request?

An Academy specialist confirms current availability and schedules one Charge project walkthrough. No registration, funding or activation happens when you submit the form.

How does Charge create revenue?

Drivers pay for completed charging sessions. Session, payment and operating records are reconciled before that revenue enters the Charge funding model.

Where are the exact plan and terms reviewed?

Academy reviews the account, plan, fees, settlement and exit with you in the client account. The exact terms are then confirmed in the written offer before activation.

Is the Clermont station owned by EcoGrid?

No. It is a public market reference from the AFDC directory, shown so you can see the physical category behind the operating model.
