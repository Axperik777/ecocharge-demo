Why this matters now

Changing fuel costs. A reason to look at charging.

Higher gasoline and diesel prices give drivers another reason to compare electric vehicles with fuel-powered cars. EVs have no tailpipe emissions. Charge focuses on the infrastructure that serves those drivers.

The opportunity is in paid charging sessions. Site usage, electricity prices and operating costs determine the project’s result.

AI

EcoGrid AI · optional service

The planned mode compares usage, electricity costs and downtime across eligible charging sites to guide allocation within the project.

The team uses tools to operate equipment. The optional member AI mode compares eligible allocations of your participation within the selected project. This is separate from normal project operation.

Welcome gift and activation terms

472 EcoCoin · 30 days

Your 500 EcoCoin welcome gift covers the first 30 days of AI mode in your selected project, leaving 28 EcoCoin.

No investment is required to receive the gift. Project AI mode requires participation in the relevant project. The period starts at activation; renewal requires confirmation.

AI mode is an optional addition to the base plan. The terms of any additional return are reviewed during the project presentation.

Proposed terms: a new period starts only with your confirmation; no coins are charged automatically. After 30 days the additional mode ends; base participation continues under its agreement. Before activation, the team must confirm how to obtain renewal coins and the full cancellation process.

AI mode is in development. Activation will become available once its terms are finalized.
