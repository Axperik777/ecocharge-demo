What you receive. Where the result comes from. What can go wrong.

  1. 01

    What you receive

    A capacity buyer receives a written GPU configuration and service quote. A participant receives a separate project brief tied to a defined site and agreement.

  2. 02

    Source of the result

    Businesses pay for measured GPU usage or reserved capacity. A project result, where offered, is calculated only after energy, cooling, network, service, downtime, replacement and reserve costs.

  3. 03

    Main risk

    Utilization, electricity, cooling, network incidents, downtime, equipment ageing, customer demand and contract terms can change the economics. No fixed result is promised.

  4. 04

    Next step

    Choose capacity or participation, describe the task and review the available configuration, site evidence, complete price and written terms.
