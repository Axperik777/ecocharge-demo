Why this matters now

Generate electricity without buying fuel to run the panels.

Solar panels do not need oil or diesel to generate electricity. That removes fuel purchases from the generation process and avoids emissions from burning fuel on site. Storage can shift when that power is used or sold.

The investment case comes from selling the power generated. Output, customer contracts, equipment costs and maintenance determine the economics.
