About EcoGrid

Project background, people and the documents behind any participation claim.

James R. Whitaker

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Read the full storyView the document register

From electrical work and the first chargers to EcoGrid: solar generation, storage, equipment and computing. One foundation — understanding how energy is produced and used.

Grid means an energy network. EcoGrid Solar, EcoGrid Storage and EcoGrid Charge connect generation, storage and energy use. EcoGrid Mining extends that infrastructure to computing. Our EcoGrid AI helps manage energy, equipment performance and operating costs across every direction. EcoCoin is a planned product direction. Its network, supported uses and any transfer process apply only after they are set out in final product documents. EcoGrid Club brings together the people who want to follow these directions, discuss projects and stay in touch with the team.

The company model connects solar generation, storage, equipment supply and installation, charging and energy for businesses. EcoGrid Mining is a separate service: clients rent computing cells on EcoGrid farms instead of buying miners. EcoGrid Solar and storage power the miners and cooling, while EcoGrid owns, operates, repairs and replaces the hardware and manages responsible end-of-life handling. Digital-asset output is variable and is defined only by the rental agreement.

Economic value and responsibility to the planet belong in one system.

EcoGrid evaluates every project through two equal lenses: how it creates economic value and what impact comes from its energy source, equipment and full life cycle.

01

Clear economics

Revenue source, costs, participation rights, reporting and risks must be understandable before a decision.

02

Measurable impact

Energy source, consumption, efficiency, equipment life and end-of-life handling require project-level data.

Energy and emissions

We prioritize renewable generation, transport without tailpipe emissions and EcoGrid Solar generation and storage for our computing farms.

Efficiency and useful life

Using energy and equipment efficiently — and keeping equipment useful for longer — can reduce avoidable demand for replacements and resources.

Repair and end of life

Electronics, panels and batteries require repair, reuse where appropriate and qualified recycling at the end of service.

Evidence and claim limits

We do not promise “no harm,” “zero emissions” or guaranteed income without supporting evidence. Goals, assumptions and verified results must remain separate for each project. IPCC: Energy systems ↗ · UNEP: Global Resources Outlook ↗ · UNEP: Electronic waste ↗

Founder and company values

EcoGrid AI: Energy decisions that support profitability

EcoGrid AI is our shared operating intelligence across every project. It compares generation, demand, tariffs and equipment condition to help allocate energy where it delivers more value and reduce avoidable costs. Operators set the limits and approve operating decisions.

EcoGrid AI: Use more of the energy we generate

EcoGrid AI forecasts solar output from weather and site data, compares it with demand and helps plan when to use, store or sell energy. This reduces unused generation and purchases during expensive hours, supporting project profitability.

EcoGrid AI · optional service

EcoGrid uses AI tools to compare solar generation, storage and demand.

This is an operating capability, not a Solar or Mining rate promise. Any client service or benefit is defined only in a separate project proposal and agreement.

Operating tool · separate project terms

EcoGrid AI: Match power to charging demand

EcoGrid AI forecasts busy charging periods and helps distribute available power between stations, batteries and other site loads. It flags unusual consumption and equipment faults so the team can reduce downtime and manage the cost of each charging session.

EcoGrid AI · optional service

When Charge AI is confirmed and active, it adds exactly +1 percentage point to the selected plan rate for 30 days. After that period, the base rate applies again.

Activation is confirmed individually for a 30-day period after the AI terms and the related payment or EcoCoin credit are verified. There is no automatic renewal. The uplift is not 1% of revenue or a payout guarantee.

Optional · confirmed individually

EcoGrid AI: Control the cost of computing

EcoGrid AI compares electricity prices, computing output, temperature and cooling demand. It helps operators choose workload schedules and spot inefficient machines early, reducing avoidable energy costs and downtime to improve the operating result.

EcoGrid AI · optional service

EcoGrid uses AI tools to compare energy, cooling and computing efficiency.

This is an operating capability, not a Solar or Mining rate promise. Any client service or benefit is defined only in a separate project proposal and agreement.
