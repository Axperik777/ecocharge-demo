How to read the Charge terms: 13–16% per calendar month and four-day settlement

Separate the fixed company obligation from station performance, funding sources and the timing of a funded settlement.

Two questions stay together

What creates economic value—and what the energy source, efficiency, equipment life and end-of-life handling mean for the full system.

The rate and the funding source answer different questions

After agreement activation, the selected Charge plan sets a fixed company obligation of 13%, 14%, 15% or 16% for a full UTC calendar month. Accrual runs continuously and is prorated for the actual time in the period.

The obligation is not described as a forecast of one public station. The separate question is how that obligation is funded and when a funded amount becomes available.

Read the sequence in the correct order

  1. 01

    The agreement fixes the obligation

    The selected plan sets the Charge-only calendar-month rate after confirmed activation.

  2. 02

    Funding is recorded

    Forty-two percent of verified Charge revenue funds participant obligations first. An eligible shortfall may be covered by the company reserve; an uncovered amount remains company payable.

  3. 03

    Funded profit is settled

    Every four days, funded accrued profit is settled and can become withdrawable. Continuous accrual continues into the next settlement period.

Questions the written terms must answer

  • Who owes the obligation and what happens to capital under the exit terms.
  • How verified Charge revenue, reserve use, shortfall and company payable are evidenced.
  • Which fees, eligibility rules, timing and counterparty risks apply.

Fixed obligation does not mean risk-free payment

Actual payment still depends on verified funding and the company’s ability to perform. Only funded, settled profit is withdrawable; the agreement must explain capital and counterparty risk.

Charge uses a fixed company obligation for a UTC calendar month, with continuous accrual and settlement every 4 days. These terms apply only to Charge and still require verified funding and the written agreement.
