Your wallet, step by step

  1. Check compatibility

    Before installing a wallet, ask the Club team which network EcoCoin uses and which wallets support it. The wallet and receiving network must match the gift instructions.

  2. Create and secure your wallet

    Install the wallet from its official source and follow its backup instructions. Recovery words or private keys must stay with you. Never send them to support or enter them in an EcoGrid form.

  3. Receive the gift

    Open Receive in your wallet, check the network and copy the public receiving address. Follow the Club’s gift instructions. A receiving address is different from your recovery words.

  4. Choose how to use it

    Check your EcoCoin balance, then review the reward and its cost. Before confirming any wallet action, check what you are authorizing and whether a network fee applies.

Free EcoGrid AI is a separate Club benefit. Using the basic AI service does not require spending EcoCoin.

Community: start a conversation

Bring a question, an idea or something you have made.+

You do not need a finished project to join in. A useful question or a small result from your AI lesson is enough to start a conversation in Telegram or Facebook comments.

Give people something to respond to

  • Say what you are working on.
  • Share what you have tried.
  • Ask one specific question.

Respond to someone else’s idea too. A conversation grows through small exchanges.

An example first comment
I am working on a page for my handmade products. I used AI to draft an introduction and made it sound more like me. What would you want to know before placing an order?
