ENERGY MODEL

How the energy flows

Model, not telemetry

The EcoGrid model combines solar generation in the U.S. and Canada and battery storage with three revenue streams: EV charging, energy supplied to shopping centers and businesses under long-term contracts below their local utility tariff, and sales of about 43% of production to the grid. The remaining 57% serves chargers and commercial clients. Battery storage shifts daytime generation into the evening and night.

About EcoGrid
Where should I start?

Start with the Charge terms, then verify the company and supporting evidence. Solar and Mining remain open for research, but are not parallel active offers in the current campaign.

What exactly is fixed?

The agreement fixes the company obligation at 13–16% per UTC calendar month. Verified Charge revenue funds it first through the 42% participant pool; the company reserve covers an eligible shortfall. Any uncovered amount remains company payable. Only funded, settled profit is withdrawable.

Review the terms and risks
What can I do in my account?

Track active Charge capital, free funds, funded settled profit, company payable, four-day settlements and verified operations. You can also contact your manager and open the supporting documents.
