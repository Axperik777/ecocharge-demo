ENERGY · ENGINEERING · RESPONSIBILITY

EcoGrid designs, builds and operates energy infrastructure.

We take energy and technology sites from load calculation and connection planning through equipment, monitoring and day-to-day operations. Solar, Charge and Compute share one engineering foundation while keeping separate commercial terms.

How EcoGrid grew ↓Facts and documents ↗

HOW WE GOT HERE

We started with electrical work, not a pitch deck.

The foundation was practical: calculate the load, plan the connection, install the equipment and remain responsible after launch. That work led to a broader question—how energy should be generated, stored, distributed and controlled around useful demand. Solar, Charge and Compute grew from that engineering logic.

WHAT GUIDES US

Convictions should be visible in conduct.

EcoGrid founder and CEO James R. (Jim) Whitaker is a Protestant Christian. His convictions guide both his personal principles and the company’s conduct: tell the truth, act lawfully and transparently, keep your word, care for your family and help people in need. EcoGrid directs part of its own available funds after expenses and obligations to charitable initiatives and Protestant church communities—not client or project funds. We welcome people of every faith and those with none.
