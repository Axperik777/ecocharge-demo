Before you choose.

Is this the price of the panels alone?

No. These are planning estimates for a system with installation, before incentives. Your written quote defines the equipment, labor, permits, taxes and any additional work.

Will the system power my home during an outage?

Solar panels alone generally do not provide backup during a grid outage. Ask about compatible battery storage and backup equipment as a separate option.

How much electricity will it produce?

Production depends on your location, roof angle, shade, weather and system design. We do not infer production or savings from system size alone.

Is a federal 30% tax credit included?

No incentive is deducted from these estimates. The IRS states that the residential clean energy credit is unavailable for property placed in service after December 31, 2025. State or utility programs depend on your location and eligibility.

Read current IRS guidance ↗
Does buying panels activate an account plan?

No. A home solar purchase is a separate equipment inquiry. It does not fund a participation plan, create accruals or trigger a Club funding bonus.
