What your written quote will show.

The quote separates included work, optional upgrades and exclusions so a low headline price cannot hide the real project cost.

01

System equipment

Modules, inverter or microinverters, mounting, monitoring and export controls.

02

Site and electrical work

Design, delivery, installation and any required service-panel or wiring upgrades.

03

Local requirements

Permits, utility review, taxes, separately stated incentives and clearly listed exclusions.

04

Storage and backup

Battery, controls, grid isolation, protected loads and the operating limits of the selected configuration.

Before you request a quote.

Is the planning reference the final installed price?

No. It is a non-binding starting reference. The final written quote must show the exact equipment, labor, delivery, permits, utility work, taxes, upgrades and exclusions for the address.

Does the address check guarantee approval or savings?

No. Service availability, permits, interconnection, production and savings depend on the property, utility, tariff, shade, system design and confirmed costs.

Can the system provide backup during an outage?

Solar panels alone generally do not provide backup during a grid outage. Compatible storage, controls and grid-isolation equipment must be designed and quoted for the required loads.

Is EcoGrid the manufacturer of every listed component?

No. EcoGrid names the system configurations. The actual OEM manufacturer and model remain visible, and manufacturer specifications and warranty terms apply.
