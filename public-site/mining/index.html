ENERGY + COMPUTING

Where mining fits EcoGrid.

In the EcoGrid model, mining is a flexible load that can use surplus solar generation when it is available. The energy source, operating schedule and financial benefit must be assessed for each site.

Energy has a cost

Solar generation varies by time and weather. Grid supply or storage must be sized and priced separately; solar panels do not make round-the-clock mining free.
