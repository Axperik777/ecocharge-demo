ENERGY + COMPUTING

Where mining fits EcoGrid.

Mining is a separate use of electricity with its own equipment economics. A site plan must cover electricity, cooling and operating costs.

Energy has a cost

Solar generation varies by time and weather. Grid supply or storage must be sized and priced separately; solar panels do not make round-the-clock mining free.

Terms before a transfer

Review the project owner, site, equipment, fees, reporting, payout timing and exit rules. Your manager provides the proposal for review.

Separate from energy plans

Energy-plan rates, weekly accruals, four-day withdrawal windows and Club rewards do not apply to a mining inquiry. Mining terms require a separate agreement.
