ENERGY + COMPUTING

Where mining fits EcoGrid.

In the EcoGrid model, mining is a flexible load that can use surplus solar generation when it is available. The energy source, operating schedule and financial benefit must be assessed for each site.

Energy has a cost

Solar generation varies by time and weather. Grid supply or storage must be sized and priced separately; solar panels do not make round-the-clock mining free.

EcoGrid AI

Control the cost of computing

EcoGrid AI compares electricity prices, computing output, temperature and cooling demand. It helps operators choose workload schedules and spot inefficient machines early, reducing avoidable energy costs and downtime to improve the operating result.

EcoGrid AI · optional service

The planned mode compares computing output, electricity, cooling and uptime across compatible equipment within the selected project. Hardware and algorithm limits remain in place.
