How EcoGrid powers its computing farms.

EcoGrid Solar generation and storage supply the miners and cooling systems. The operating schedule balances available energy, cell efficiency, temperature and service requirements.

Our energy system

Solar generation charges EcoGrid storage; stored energy supports computing and cooling when generation changes. The proposal identifies the farm and its operating assumptions.

The client rents a cell

The miner remains EcoGrid equipment. The client does not take ownership, arrange delivery or operate the machine.

The agreement defines the result

The rental proposal records the cell, algorithm, term, service, costs, settlement, downtime and exit rules.

EcoGrid AI: Control the cost of computing

EcoGrid AI compares electricity prices, computing output, temperature and cooling demand. It helps operators choose workload schedules and spot inefficient machines early, reducing avoidable energy costs and downtime to improve the operating result.
