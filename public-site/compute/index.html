HOW THE PARTICIPANT RESULT IS FORMED

Customers pay for AI computing. The project covers its costs. The remaining result is allocated under the agreement.

Participation is tied to one defined infrastructure project. The proposal explains what is financed, who buys the service, which costs come first and what part of the variable result relates to the participant.

  1. 01
    Fix the participation terms

    The written proposal defines the infrastructure, participant rights and use of funds.

  2. 02
    Customers pay for computing

    Business customers pay for computing workloads delivered by the project.

  3. 03
    Cover all project costs

    Energy, cooling, network, service, tax, downtime, replacement and reserves come first.

  4. 04
    Allocate the variable result

    The agreement determines what part relates to the participant, how it is reported and how exit works.

Paid AI computing − all project costs = variable result; the agreement defines the participant’s part

If paid demand is lower or costs are higher, the result falls and can be negative. Capital remains at risk.

WHY COMPUTE EXISTS

Compute continues EcoGrid’s engineering logic—it is not a separate GPU story.

Founder and CEO James R. (Jim) Whitaker built the company from practical electrical work. Each next direction added a layer that the next one can use: sites and connections, distributed loads, generation and storage, then a managed computing service.

  1. 01
    Electrical foundation

    Site power, connections, safety and field service came first.

  2. 02
    EcoGrid Charge

    Charge added experience with distributed loads, uptime and customer-facing energy service.

  3. 03
    EcoGrid Solar

    Solar and storage add generation, metering and peak management where a site design confirms them.

  4. 04
    EcoGrid Compute

    Compute can turn managed power into a computing service sold to customers with real workloads.

How Compute strengthens the company model

Where engineering and contracts allow shared infrastructure, Compute can add a measurable load for generation, storage, grid connections and the operating team. This can improve how those resources are used without merging the three products.

HOW VALUE IS CREATED

Five links from energy to collected customer revenue.

  1. 01
    Solar and storage

    The site design determines what can be generated, stored and used locally.

  2. 02
    Power and backup

    Available capacity, tariff, backup and operating limits are checked for the specific site.

  3. 03
    Cooling and network

    Heat removal, connectivity and service access define safe operating density.

  4. 04
    GPU service

    The equipment class and configuration follow the customer task and site limits.

  5. 05
    Paid workload

    Revenue begins only after a customer pays for the delivered computing service.

One engineering base. Separate products.

Solar and storage can help manage load where technically feasible, but they do not mean continuous operation from sunlight alone. Charge, Solar and Compute may share site components and operating expertise; each direction keeps its own customers, revenue, costs, risks, application, proposal and agreement.

AI capacity depends on energy, cooling and responsible operations.

EcoGrid Compute starts with the workload, then reviews accelerator class, power, cooling, network, monitoring and service. The actual energy mix and equipment life cycle must be confirmed for each site.
