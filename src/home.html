BEFORE YOU EXPLORE

A few things to know.

Do I receive a separate payment for every car?

In this demo, charging sessions illustrate where operating revenue comes from. Your account calculation is weekly: invested capital × the selected weekly rate. We do not add a second payment for every vehicle.

What does the 43% figure mean?

It is an assumption in the proposed company participation model. Its network scope and supporting ownership documents have not been provided. It does not mean 43% of all US charging stations.

Can I deposit or withdraw real money?

No. You can submit demo requests and have them reviewed in the team workspace. Balances and messages are stored only in this browser.

Are the stations and photos real?

The directory contains public US charging locations and source-linked location photos. A directory entry does not establish company ownership or an investment right.
